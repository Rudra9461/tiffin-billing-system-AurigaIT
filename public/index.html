🍱 Tiffin Billing System

Fair, automatic, pro-rated billing for home-style tiffin services.

What is this?

A simple subscription and billing manager built for home-style tiffin (lunch delivery) services. Customers subscribe to a monthly plan and get lunch delivered on weekdays. When life happens — travel, festivals, sick days — customers can pause service, and they are never charged for the days they didn't receive a tiffin. At month-end, the owner gets an accurate, verifiable, pro-rated bill for every customer.

Key Features

📋 Subscribe & Manage

Add customers and subscribe them to a monthly plan in seconds.

⏸️ Pause & Resume

Log travel or festival pauses — paused weekdays are never billed.

💰 Accurate Pro-Rated Billing

Bill = daily rate × actual weekdays served. Fully transparent and verifiable.

🔍 Search by Phone

Look up any customer instantly by phone number.

🟢 Active vs Paused View

See at a glance who's currently being served and who's paused.

📊 Sortable, Paginated List

Manage large customer lists easily with sorting and pagination.

Who is this for?

Small and local home-style tiffin service owners — the kind who run their business over WhatsApp and a notebook today — who deliver lunch to 10 to 500+ customers and need a simple way to track subscriptions, pauses, and monthly billing without spreadsheets or manual math.

How it helps

Manual billing after pauses is error-prone and leads to disputes ("I was traveling for 5 days, why was I charged full price?"). This system removes that friction entirely — every pause is logged with dates, every bill is calculated from that history, and the owner can show the customer exactly how their bill was calculated: eligible weekdays, paused weekdays, and the resulting amount.
